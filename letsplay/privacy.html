Let's Play

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version

Let's Play does not collect any data. Nothing your child does in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The stickers your child collects, the settings in the grown-ups corner and the record of which games have had their free play are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes the stickers and settings.

No internet, accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Let's Play does not connect to the internet. There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no notifications, and the app contains no third-party code.

Made for young children

Let's Play is made for children aged five and under. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no personal information from children. There are no links out of the app for children, and nothing a child can buy.

The grown-ups corner, purchases and this page sit behind a grown-up question: typing a number written out in words.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your purchase on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
