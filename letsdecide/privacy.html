Let's Decide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version

Let's Decide does not collect any data. Nothing you make in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your lists, the options in them, any photos or emoji you add, and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party code and does not connect to the internet.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a picture to an option, the app asks to use the camera or lets you pick a photo. The picture is saved only with that list on your device.

Sharing into the app

You can share text from other apps into Let's Decide to make a new list. That text is passed directly to the app on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your purchase on your device.

Children

Let's Decide is designed for families. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no personal information from children. Purchases and Settings sit behind a grown-up question.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
